# Small Group Curriculum

A whole study, built for the leaders you actually have.

> Requires: pastor-foundation skill
> **Tier 3.** Drafts fully. This is leader material, not a sermon.

Different from `small-group-questions`, which turns one sermon into one week of
discussion. This builds a whole study that stands on its own.

---

## What You Need to Provide

**Required:**
- Book of the Bible, topic, or sermon series to build from
- Number of weeks

**Helpful:**
- Who the groups are: new believers, long-timers, mixed, men, women, couples, young
  adults, recovery
- How experienced the leaders are. This changes everything about how much scaffolding
  the guide needs.
- Session length
- Whether groups do homework between sessions (most do not, plan accordingly)
- Whether this runs parallel to the sermon series or independently

**Minimum to start:** the topic and the number of weeks.

---

## Step 1: Scope Check

Before building, assess honestly whether the material fits the weeks.

Say so if it does not. Six weeks in Romans is a survey, not a study, and pretending
otherwise produces a guide that skims. Recommend narrowing or extending.

Right-size to `ATTENDANCE`. A church with four groups and volunteer leaders needs a
different guide than one with twenty groups and trained facilitators. Assume
volunteers who are reading the guide for the first time twenty minutes before group.

**Output:** one paragraph. If the premise needs adjusting, say it before building.

---

## Step 2: Arc

Map the whole study before writing any session. For each week: the passage, the single
idea, and how it moves from the week before.

A study needs a shape. Week 1 is not just "the first chapter." Name what the arc is
doing: building a foundation, escalating tension, working through objections, moving
from understanding to practice.

**Output:** a table of all weeks with passage, session title, and one-line idea.

---

## Step 3: Build Each Session

Every session gets the same structure so leaders learn the rhythm:

**Leader Prep** (what the leader needs before group)
- The single idea in one sentence
- 3-4 sentences of context the leader needs but will not read aloud
- The one thing likely to derail this discussion, and how to handle it
- Time to prepare: be honest, and keep it under 20 minutes

**Opening** (5-10 min)
- One warmup question that connects to the topic without announcing it

**In the Text** (15-20 min)
- The passage, with reading instructions (who reads, how much)
- 2-3 observation questions: what does it say
- 2-3 interpretation questions: what does it mean

**So What** (15-20 min)
- 3-4 application questions that are specific enough to be answerable
- At least one that is uncomfortable

**Close** (5-10 min)
- A prayer prompt tied to the session
- One concrete thing to carry into the week

**Leader Notes**
- Likely answers to the harder questions, so a leader is not caught flat
- What to do if nobody talks
- What to do if one person talks the whole time
- When to let silence sit

---

## Step 4: Question Quality

Every question gets checked against this before it ships:

- **Not answerable with yes or no.** Rewrite it.
- **Not a quiz.** If there is one right answer the leader is fishing for, it is a bad
  discussion question.
- **Not so open it floats.** "What does this passage mean to you" produces nothing.
- **Application is specific.** "How can you love others better this week" is a wasted
  question. Name a situation.
- **At least one question per session risks something.** Groups that only trade safe
  answers are not doing anything.

---

## Step 5: Leader Onboarding

One page at the front for leaders, especially new ones:

- How to use the guide, and permission to cut material
- Running long is normal. What to cut when it happens.
- Handling the person who derails, the person who never speaks, the wrong answer, and
  the question you cannot answer
- When to escalate something to the pastor. Be specific: disclosed abuse, crisis, a
  group member in real trouble, persistent doctrinal conflict.

That escalation list is the most important thing on the page.

---

## Output Format

Markdown to `output/`, one file for the whole study:

```
output/Curriculum-James-6-weeks-2026-08-02.md
```

No PDF by default. These get edited, reordered, and printed per group. If a print
version is wanted, say so and it can be added.

---

## Quality Bar

**Done looks like:** a volunteer leader with twenty minutes of prep runs a good group
without calling you.

**Failure modes:**
- Questions only a seminary graduate would ask
- More material than fits the session. Leaders will not cut; they will rush.
- Leader notes that assume knowledge the leader does not have
- Application so generic it could attach to any passage
- Missing the escalation guidance
